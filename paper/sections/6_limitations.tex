\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The following limitations apply to the revision as it stands.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Four assigned anchors.}
  The core fit uses four points and two parameters ($\mathrm{dof}=2$).
  The covariance and the leave-one-anchor-out tests are not yet reported.
  The notebook inputs and the submitted prose name different quartets.
  \revtodo{Confirm the anchor list and add the covariance and leave-one-out table.}

  \item \textbf{Interpretation of the time coordinate.}
  Present-day $H_0$ inferences are assigned to earlier cosmic times in the figure notebook.
  The Planck entry is a $\Lambda$CDM inference of today's Hubble constant.
  The curve is not a demonstrated expansion history.

  \item \textbf{Normalization and units.}
  The Hubble notebooks use $\beta\,10^{5}\xi(t)$.
  The submitted unscaled equation does not reproduce the quoted $H(z)$.
  The relation between $H_\infty$ and $\Lambda_\infty$ is dimensionally unsettled, and the $10^{5}$ factor has not been fixed for $\gamma$.
  \revtodo{One constant table and one normalization for $H$, $\Lambda$, and $\alpha$.}

  \item \textbf{Quasar coefficients are not reconciled.}
  The submitted text quotes both $+6.42\pm 1.14$ and $+5.56\pm 0.99$ for the Murphy sample, and the Keck-only and VLT-only subsets disagree in sign.
  There is no pre-registered subset rule.
  The comparison is exploratory.

  \item \textbf{Late-time goodness of fit.}
  On the quoted numbers, the ansatz is a worse fit than flat $\Lambda$CDM on the samples in \cref{tab:dense}, and the DESI DR2 entry is of order $10^3$ in $\chi^2/\mathrm{dof}$.
  A common-likelihood AIC/BIC comparison has not been rerun.

  \item \textbf{Stability.}
  The five background checks previously reported do not cover perturbative stability, gradient instabilities, or the origin of the prescribed sound speed.

  \item \textbf{Forecast.}
  The high-redshift mock uses independent fractional errors, a $\Lambda$CDM clock, and $\sqrt{\Delta\chi^2}$ as a significance.
  It is not a survey forecast.

  \item \textbf{External clock.}
  Evaluating $\xi(t(z))$ with a flat $\Lambda$CDM age builds a $\Lambda$CDM assumption into the time coordinate of an ansatz that is then compared with $\Lambda$CDM.

  \item \textbf{Scope that was removed.}
  No lattice CMB spectrum, inverse reconstruction, or acoustic freeze-out result is available in this revision, and none is cited as support.
\end{enumerate}
